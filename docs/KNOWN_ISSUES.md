| 9 | Clone, fee model | Resolved 2026-09-25 (milestone 5): permissionless `reconcile(orderId)` settles a Pending order from GMX state once GMX no longer holds it; verified on a fork with a real lost callback (1-gas callback budget). | — | `test_lostExecutionCallback_*`, `test_lostCancellationCallback_*`. |
| 10 | Adapter markets | Resolved 2026-09-30: markets are configured with their index, long and short tokens; collateral is any pool token (e.g. USDC.SG on every Sepolia market); the verifier prices each token from its own Chainlink feed with its own max age; leverage, fee and GMX's estimate use the right token's price and decimals. | — | `SealedOrderAdapterMarkets.t.sol`; fork `SealedBtcUsdcDemo.t.sol` (BTC long with USDC collateral on live GMX). |
| 11 | Trigger checks | Resolved 2026-09-30: per-check fee paid from each order's check budget (ETH, locked at intake, topped up by the owner, unused part freed on fill or cancel); the checker gets `checkFee - checkFeeSpread`, the fee collector the spread. Sepolia: 0.00005 ETH per check, 10% spread. | — | `SealedOrderAdapterCheckFee.t.sol`. |
| 12 | Reconcile | If a callback is lost and the position then shrinks before anyone reconciles (liquidation, ADL), reconcile sees neither "grew" nor "collateral back" and refuses (`CannotReconcile`); the order stays Pending and its lock stuck. Manual closes can't cause this (blocked while adapter orders are in flight). | Low likelihood (needs a lost callback plus a liquidation first). Options: record GMX's position `increasedAtTime` at submit, or an owner-only override that settles as Executed without fee. | Liquidate the position between a lost-callback fill and reconcile. |
| 13 | Clone | Only one manual close can be in flight at a time, and adapter submits wait for it to leave GMX's order list. | Acceptable for v1; revisit if users need concurrent manual orders. | — |
| 14 | Execution pricing | Resolved 2026-09-30. GMX validates `acceptablePrice` against an execution price that includes the trade's own price impact. On the imbalanced Sepolia ETH/USD pool a $100 long close was priced ~15% under the oracle, so slippage bounds computed from the oracle price failed. The adapter now asks GMX's Reader (`getExecutionPrice`, the same calculation GMX validates against) for the expected fill at the check price, and applies the user's slippage around that, for increases and decreases. | — | `test_execute_acceptablePriceAnchoredOnGmxExecutionEstimate`; fork stop-loss demo now runs with real price impact. |
| 16 | Checker | The reference checker processes orders one by one (reads, decrypts and executes serially), keeps no state across restarts beyond re-scanning `OrderSealed` logs, and decrypts every checked order even when nothing fired. Fine for a demo, slow with many orders. | Batch reads with multicall, decrypt in parallel, persist the scan cursor. Anyone can run a better checker; the contracts don't depend on this one. | — |
| 17 | Checker operations | Live run 2026-09-30: with the checker and the user on the same key, a client `submitOrder` and a checker `execute` raced for the same nonce and the `execute` failed. The order recovered on the next feed round. | Run the checker on its own key (`CHECKER_PRIVATE_KEY`); have the checker retry `execute` for checked-but-unexecuted fired orders instead of waiting for a new check. | — |
| 18 | Check budget | An order whose budget runs out stops being checked, silently from the chain's point of view (the checker skips it). With Sepolia's ETH/USD feed updating every 30–120 s, 0.001 ETH lasts 20 checks, i.e. well under an hour if every update is checked. | Client shows checks left; a front end should warn and offer top-ups. On mainnet, feeds update on a 0.05% deviation or a 24 h heartbeat, so a budget lasts far longer. Could add a per-order maximum check frequency so users trade reaction speed for cost. | — |
| 19 | Check fee | The fee is a fixed ETH amount set at deployment, while gas prices vary. If gas rises well above ~0.07 gwei (Sepolia sizing), checking costs checkers more than they're paid, and permissionless checking may stall. | Size mainnet's fee from its gas history with headroom; or pay the checker `gasUsed × tx.gasprice` capped per order, at the cost of more logic. | — |
