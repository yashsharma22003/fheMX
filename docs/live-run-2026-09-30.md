## Measurements (answers to design §10)

Gas price during the run: 0.03–0.06 gwei.

| Step | Gas | Wall time |
| --- | --- | --- |

| `submitOrder`, later orders | 997,541 – 1,040,678 | — |
| `checkBatch`, 1 order | 662,042 – 747,396 | 3.4–4.0 s to confirm |
| `checkBatch`, 2 orders | 1,129,495 (565k per order) | 3.8 s |
| CoFHE `decryptForTx` (Teecryptor) per value | — | 0.64–0.98 s |
| `execute` (publish 3 decryptions + GMX order) | 1,542,293 – 1,601,889 | 3.6–3.9 s |
| GMX keeper execution | — | ≤ 15 s after `execute` (bounded by the checker's 10 s poll) |
| `settle` | 66,736 – 73,263 | 3.3–3.4 s |
| `cancelOrder` | 63,026 | — |

**Latency, trigger to GMX order:** one feed update (30–120 s on Sepolia) + one checker poll (10 s) + check (~3.5 s) + decrypt (~1 s per value, only size for non-fired orders) + execute (~3.7 s). With a fresh feed answer, a fired order reached GMX in about 20 s; GMX filled it within another ~15 s.

**Cost:** a single-order check is ≈ 700k gas ≈ 0.00004 ETH at 0.05 gwei; batching two orders cut the per-order check cost by ~25%. A full lifecycle (submit, one fired check, execute, settle) ≈ 3.5M gas ≈ 0.0002 ETH, plus GMX's execution fee. The whole run, including deployment, used about 0.003 ETH.

**Decrypt:** the zero-unless-fired pattern works live. Non-fired checks decrypt only the size (~0.65 s); the trigger price is never decrypted.

**Keeper liveness:** GMX Sepolia keepers executed both orders within one checker poll.

## Issues found

- **Nonce clash when the checker shares the user's key** (known issue 17). The client's `submitOrder` and the checker's `execute` were sent from the same account at the same moment; one failed with a stale nonce. No funds were affected and the order recovered on the next feed round, but a production checker needs its own key.
- The reference checker only executes right after a check; a fired-but-unexecuted order waits for the next feed update (known issue 16).

## Still open

- Per-check fee (known issue 11): now sizeable from the numbers above.
- Longer soak test of feed freshness and keeper liveness.
- Mainnet parameters (design §10: hard caps, max report age vs. Arbitrum One's 24 h heartbeat, sequencer-uptime feed).
